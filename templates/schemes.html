These schemes are relevant to sanitation, cleanliness, sewage management and the safety of sanitation workers.

Swachh Bharat Mission – Grameen

Supports sanitation and cleanliness in rural areas, including toilet usage and solid and liquid waste management.

  • Rural sanitation
  • Solid and liquid waste management
  • Sustaining ODF villages
